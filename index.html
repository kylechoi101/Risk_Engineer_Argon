ABOUT THIS MODEL

Built to explore, not certify.

The 3D buildings and street centerlines come from public DataSF datasets. The coastline and bridge illustration are schematic. Hazard nodes, dependencies, failure curves, asset costs, casualty factors, and retrofit effects are illustrative assumptions created for this demo. They are not facility records or an engineering forecast.

The water slider is a simplified coastal water-level proxy for tide, surge, or tsunami run-up; it does not simulate wave physics or arrival time. The engine runs 4,096 seeded Monte Carlo samples in your browser and pairs baseline and mitigated draws. VaR₀.₉₉ is the 99th percentile modeled loss; CVaR₀.₉₉ is the average loss beyond that threshold. Each fix card shows its standalone modeled impact at the current hazard settings. ROI is modeled expected loss avoided divided by CapEx for this scenario, not an annualized return.

No blueprints, SCADA configurations, inspection videos, or live sensors are connected. An engineer would need verified asset inventories, fragility curves, exposure estimates, and calibration before using outputs for decisions.
