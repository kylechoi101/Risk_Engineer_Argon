ABOUT THIS MODEL

Built to explore, not certify.

DataSF supplies 3D building footprints, ground and roof elevations, streets, tall-building structural inventory, soft-story property points, and seismic hazard-zone boundaries. NOAA station 9414290 supplies the MHHW elevation in NAVD88. The coastline and bridge illustration are schematic.

The displayed low-ground count compares footprint ground elevation with MHHW plus the selected water-level increment. It does not model barriers, hydraulic connectivity, waves, or overtopping. Mapped seismic-zone overlap, tall and soft-story inventory counts, and low-ground count make small bounded adjustments to assumed node failure probabilities. These adjustments are scenario proxies, not calibrated fragility curves. Hazard nodes, dependencies, asset costs, casualties, and retrofit effects remain illustrative assumptions.

The animated seismic rings, flood sheet, and failure beacons are visual effects. The engine runs 4,096 seeded Monte Carlo samples in your browser. VaR₀.₉₉ is the 99th percentile modeled loss; CVaR₀.₉₉ is mean loss in the highest 1% tail. ROI is scenario expected loss avoided divided by CapEx, not an annualized return.

USGS ShakeMap scenarios, 3DEP/CUDEM terrain and bathymetry, Assessor tax roll, FEMA NSI, seawall engineering reports, utility topology, chemical inventories, blueprints, SCADA data, inspection video, and live sensors are not connected. Verified facility records and calibration are required before using this for engineering or emergency decisions.
